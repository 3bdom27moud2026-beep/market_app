## Company Profile

## Service Features
1. **ShopGuide Card**: Exclusive discounts and savings across partnered merchants and service providers.
2. **Multi-Merchant Cart**: Add products from various merchants into a single unified cart (`/v1/cart`).
3. **Location Coverage**: Delivery across Governorates, Centers, and Areas in Egypt (`/v1/locations`).
