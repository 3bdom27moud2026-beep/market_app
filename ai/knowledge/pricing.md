# Pricing & Discount Policy Reference

## Overview
Reference guidelines for product pricing, tax calculations, promotional discounts, and promo code validation.

---

## Discount Rules
- Maximum promotional discount: 25% without manager override.
- Promo codes must be active in backend database before applying to support orders.
- Taxes calculated based on customer shipping location.
