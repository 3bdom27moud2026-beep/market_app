# Refund Policy Knowledge Base

## Overview
Authoritative terms, conditions, and eligibility rules for customer returns, exchanges, and order refunds.

---

## Standard Refund Eligibility

| Condition | Refund Percentage | Processing Time | Return Shipping |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Unopened original box (within 30 days) | 100% Full Refund | 3–5 Business Days | Free |
| Opened item, non-defective | 80% Partial Refund | 3–5 Business Days | Customer Pays |
| Defective / Damaged on arrival | 100% + Replacement | Instant / 24 Hours | Free |
