# Travel Domain Knowledge Base

## Overview
Static reference knowledge about travel terminology, transport modes, accommodation categories, and travel planning conventions used across the AI platform.

---

## Transport Modes

| Mode | Best For | Speed | Booking Lead Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| International Flight | Intercontinental travel | Fast | 1-12 weeks |
| Domestic Flight | Country-level travel | Fast | 1-4 weeks |
| High-Speed Rail | City pairs ≤ 800km | Moderate | Days to weeks |
| Ferry | Island access, scenic routes | Slow | Same day to 1 week |
| Bus/Coach | Budget, scenic routes | Slow | Same day |
| Ride-hailing / Car | Last-mile access | Fast | On demand |

## Accommodation Categories

| Category | Typical Price/Night (USD) | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hostel / Dorm | $10–$30 | Social, budget |
| Budget Hotel | $30–$80 | Private rooms, minimal service |
| Mid-Range Hotel | $80–$200 | Breakfast, amenities |
| Boutique Hotel | $150–$400 | Unique design, curated experience |
| Luxury / 5-Star | $300+ | Full concierge, spa |
| Villa / Resort | $500+ | Private pool, butler service |

---

## Travel Planning Lead Times

- **International trip**: Plan 4–12 weeks in advance.
- **Weekend domestic trip**: 1–2 weeks.
- **Same-day events**: Availability varies; confirm within 24 hours.
